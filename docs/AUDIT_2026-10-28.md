blockers and the launch checklist for 1 December 2026.

**Codes whose HS6 prefix does not exist:** `2001190000` (Pickled Cucumber, Mixed pickled, Beetroot): heading 2001 has
only 2001.10 and 2001.90.

**Insufficient product information:** Egyptian Melon Seeds (raw → 1207.70, roasted → 2008.19), Crushed Red Pepper
(paste → 2103.90, dry flakes → 0904.22), Green Peas (dried or frozen), Peeled tomatoes (English name missing).

**Shared codes:** `2001190000` ×3, `2001900020` ×4, `2103900020` ×4. This is acceptable if the national tariff
line really covers them all. Three rows have no row number (Black Olive, Soya Sauce, peeled tomatoes).

**Recipe-dependent choices:** olives, artichoke and makdous fall under 2001.90 (in vinegar) per the client. If they
are packed in brine without vinegar, 2005.70 / 2005.99 apply, and the market statistics change accordingly.

**In the list but not in the catalogue PDF (19 products added, marked as such):** Dried Molokhia ("Dry Mellow"),
Sour Grapes, Egyptian Melon Seeds, Amba, Mushroom Slices, Pickled Cherry Peppers, Soya, Burger, Chilli, Ranch and
Toum sauces, Tomato Pulp, Peeled Tomatoes, Chicken Stock, Green Peas, Peas + Carrots, Frozen Beans, Okra, Fried
Potatoes.

**In the catalogue but without a client code:** priority lines olive oil, pomegranate syrup and date syrup, plus
jams, tuna, sardines, luncheon meat, halawa, tahina, Arabic sweets, pasta, legumes, rice, spices, honey and
flower waters. These keep the analyst's candidate code, and one clarification asks the client for the codes.

## 6. Blockers and client clarifications

Needed from the client (also listed in the app under *Questions & entities*):

1. The list of 85 countries, each marked existing or expansion (CSV or Excel).
2. HS codes for olive oil, pomegranate syrup, date syrup and the other catalogue lines without one.
3. Confirmation of the 11 conflicting codes and the 2001.19 prefix (Section 2), ideally from their customs broker.
4. Product details: raw or roasted melon seeds; paste or flakes for crushed red pepper; dried or frozen peas; the
   English name for peeled tomatoes; brine or vinegar for olives, artichokes and makdous.
5. The "three countries" of manufacture, and whether "Molokai" is Dried Molokhia made by Bertaya in Egypt.
6. Legal identity of Bertaya Food Industries and "Lavant company" (spelling, role, registration).
7. Identity of "Hiens company" (Heinz?) and of Hadaek Shtora.
8. Which Walmart and Lulu banners and countries to target, and the client's contact at Lulu for supplier onboarding.
9. Ingredients, processing and manufacturing country per product (fields are ready).

Needed from the platform owner (Concilio / Sparsh):

* Approval to apply the migration on Neon (it runs automatically on the next Railway deploy).
* Optional, paid: a shipment-records provider (e.g. ImportGenius, Panjiva) and a freight-rate API, for
  company-level purchase volumes and landed-cost estimates. These remain "not connected" without them.
